Terms & Conditions

1. Orders & Payment

All orders are confirmed via call or WhatsApp on 0731 515492. Payment is made on delivery for Kitengela customers, or via M-Pesa before delivery for other arrangements.

We offer deliveries within Kitengela only at this time. Delivery fees range from Ksh 100-300 depending on location. Same-day delivery is subject to order time and stock availability.
